About Me

I am an adjunct faculty member teaching CSS:100, a course that brings together computational thinking, technical writing, and practical software development.

Through a company simulation, students practice Git and GitHub, develop portfolios, and share their work with their peers.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe students learn best when they can connect foundational concepts to meaningful, hands-on work. I use practical projects to help students build confidence in problem-solving and communicate their reasoning clearly.

My approach emphasizes collaboration, constructive feedback, and reflection, helping students develop both technical competence and habits that support continued learning.
